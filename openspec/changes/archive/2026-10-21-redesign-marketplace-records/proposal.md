## Why

The browse route now uses the shared visual system, while public resource,
revision, registry-history, and failure surfaces still use the previous panel
hierarchy and narrower content treatment.

## What Changes

- Align public record routes to the shared Marketplace content edges.
- Apply one flat hierarchy to record headers, installation, tabs, and content.
- Restyle registry history and public loading, unavailable, and error states.
- Preserve identity, provenance, status, copy, tabs, installation, safe
  rendering, and recovery behavior.

## Impact

The change affects public record presentation only. APIs, persisted data, CLI
behavior, Marketplace decisions, and administration workflows do not change.
