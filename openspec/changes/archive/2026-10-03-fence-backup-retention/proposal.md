## Why

Ordinary archive retention removes names chosen by timestamp without admitting their opened shape or retaining their generation. Target discovery and removal re-resolve paths independently.

## What Changes

- Enumerate backup targets and ordinary archive names through captured directories.
- Preserve the existing day/per-day retention ordering while planning selected removals before mutation.
- Preflight selected regular single-link archive generations with shared nonblocking opened validation, then revalidate each generation and named parent before descriptor-relative unlink.
- Retain only metadata identities across the plan, closing member descriptors per inspection.

### Modified Capabilities

- `backup-recovery`: ordinary archive retention authority.

## Impact

R2 child of #508. Genuine absent backup root and current initial directory alias admission remain supported; selected unsafe archives refuse before deletion.
