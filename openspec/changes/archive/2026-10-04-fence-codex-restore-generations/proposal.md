## Why

Codex set restore preflights archives, then reopens their public names for extraction without retaining admitted member generations. Replacement archives can therefore authorize native writes after preflight; selected manifest/directory/root-key authority is also not revalidated at action boundaries.

## What Changes

- Retain one captured set directory through selection, preflight and extraction.
- Keep captured archive destinations bounded by the declared native partition count, reusing their regular-file reads for both preflight and extraction.
- Revalidate authorizing manifest generation, parent, source members and canonical output-root keys before every partition mutation.
- Log meaningful preflight completion with a structured phase for operation observability and deterministic public consumer controls.
