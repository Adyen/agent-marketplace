## Why

Telemetry has tested policy, routing, privacy, and local-control behavior but
no authoritative capability spec. Its historical document mixes these
contracts with obsolete consumer examples and dated measurements.

## What Changes

- Baseline existing telemetry policy, delivery, inventory, diagnostic
  sessions, and local controls without changing production behavior.
- Bind scenarios to current tests and strengthen retention, reset, and
  diagnostic queue witnesses where necessary.
- Delete the historical telemetry document and complete its harvest row.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `telemetry`: user and organization policy, disclosures, routes, and controls.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Specification and test evidence only. Public telemetry reference, privacy
explanation, and configuration guide remain available.
