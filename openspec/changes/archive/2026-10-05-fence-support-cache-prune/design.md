## Context

Public Run prepares a compiled cache, prunes previous support, copies source support and installs the native cache-backed link. Source admission was already captured, but prune recursively reopened cache paths and removed path-based members.

## Decisions

Capture the existing cache with fsboundary.OpenDir at prune admission. Reuse readSupportSourceDirectory and the captured NativeRoot removal operations. Verify named root device/inode before discovery and each removal; refuse ErrGenerationConflict on retarget. Relative traversal refuses aliased intermediate components. Keep compiled artifacts and their required directories, fully managed support deletion, missing-cache compatibility and initial selected-root aliases. A structured debug operation boundary carries cache_dir/phase without payloads; it also makes the real read-to-action boundary observable in the consumer regression. No new callback API/framework or dependency. No whole-tree transaction or atomic exclusion of uncooperative writers claimed.
