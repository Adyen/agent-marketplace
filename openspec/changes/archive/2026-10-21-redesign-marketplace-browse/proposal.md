## Why

Marketplace now shares the browser foundations, but its browse route still
uses narrower content edges, mixed typography, and a visually fragmented
catalog. The resource index should prioritize resources while remaining
consistent with Landing and Docs.

## What Changes

- Align Marketplace navigation, catalog, and footer to the Landing/Docs edges.
- Apply a compact section introduction and flat catalog hierarchy.
- Normalize browse typography, filters, rows, actions, counts, and states.
- Preserve search, URL filters, pagination, routing, copying, availability,
  animation, and safe text rendering.

## Impact

The change affects only public browse presentation and its automated
witnesses. APIs, persisted data, CLI behavior, and Marketplace decisions do
not change.
